ABSTRACT 
The rapid growth of digital information within modern organizations has significantly 
increased the need for intelligent systems capable of retrieving accurate information from 
large volumes of unstructured documents. Bharat Coking Coal Limited (BCCL), a 
subsidiary of Coal India Limited, maintains numerous official documents containing 
organizational rules, regulations, policies, and procedures. Retrieving specific 
information from these documents through conventional manual searching is often time-
consuming and inefficient. 
The primary objective of this internship project was to design and develop an Enterprise 
AI-Powered Knowledge Retrieval System capable of providing accurate and context-
aware responses to natural language queries based on official BCCL documents. The 
proposed solution leverages Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) to combine 
Large Language Models (LLMs) with document-based retrieval, improving response 
reliability. 
The system was implemented using the Botpress AI platform, where official PDF 
documents containing the BCCL Conduct, Discipline and Appeal (CDA) Rules were 
processed to create a searchable knowledge base. Optical Character Recognition (OCR) 
was used where required to extract text from scanned documents. An AI workflow was 
configured to retrieve relevant content and generate concise responses using prompt 
engineering. Additional improvements included response formatting, UI customization, 
and testing with real-world queries. 
The developed system demonstrates how Artificial Intelligence can enhance enterprise 
knowledge management by enabling quick access to information through natural 
language interaction. It improves accessibility, reduces search time, and ensures 
responses are based on official documents. 
This internship provided practical exposure to Enterprise AI, Natural Language 
Processing (NLP), Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), Prompt Engineering, 
LLMs, and OCR-based document processing, while strengthening technical and 
problem-solving skills.

Large Language Models (LLMs), Natural Language Processing (NLP), Prompt 
Engineering, Botpress, Knowledge Retrieval, Document Intelligence, OCR, Enterprise

1.1 Problem Statement 
Bharat Coking Coal Limited (BCCL) maintains a large collection of official documents 
containing employee conduct rules, disciplinary procedures, organizational policies, and 
administrative regulations. These documents play a crucial role in the day-to-day

administrative staff. Although these documents are available in digital format, retrieving 
specific information from them remains a challenging and time-consuming task due to

management.

it has become possible to develop intelligent systems capable of understanding user 
queries expressed in natural language. Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) further 
enhances this capability by retrieving relevant information from official documents 
before generating responses, ensuring that answers remain accurate, context-aware, and 
grounded in authentic organizational knowledge. 
Therefore, there is a need for an Enterprise AI-powered Knowledge Retrieval System that 
can process official BCCL documents, understand user queries, retrieve relevant 
information, and generate concise and reliable responses. Such a system can significantly 
reduce search time, improve information accessibility, and enhance overall operational 
efficiency within the organization.

2. SYSTEM ANALYSIS 
2.1 Existing System 
Before the development of the proposed solution, information retrieval from official 
BCCL documents was primarily carried out through manual document searching. 
Employees had to refer to lengthy PDF documents containing the Conduct, Discipline 
and Appeal (CDA) Rules, organizational policies, administrative circulars, and 
procedural guidelines whenever clarification regarding a particular rule or regulation was 
required. 
Although these documents were available in digital format, accessing specific 
information was often difficult due to their large size and complex structure. Users were 
required to manually browse through numerous pages or use conventional keyword 
search functionality provided by PDF readers. This approach depended heavily on the 
user's familiarity with the document structure and the exact terminology used within the 
document. 
For example, if an employee wanted information regarding suspension procedures or 
disciplinary penalties, they first needed to identify the appropriate document and then 
manually search for the relevant section. If different terminology was used in the search 
query, the required information might not be located efficiently. This increased the time 
required for information retrieval and reduced overall productivity. 
Another challenge involved scanned PDF documents. Since scanned files contain images 
rather than machine-readable text, conventional search methods cannot directly retrieve 
information unless Optical Character Recognition (OCR) techniques are first applied. 
Consequently, users often faced additional difficulties while searching through scanned 
documents. 
Overall, the traditional approach lacked intelligence, contextual understanding, and 
conversational interaction. It required significant manual effort and did not provide 
concise or user-friendly responses to employee queries. 
2.2 Limitations of the Existing System 
The existing document retrieval process suffers from several limitations that affect both 
efficiency and user experience. 
Firstly, manual searching through lengthy policy documents is time-consuming and often 
delays access to important information. Employees may need to examine multiple pages 
before locating the required rule or regulation.

Secondly, traditional keyword-based searching depends on exact word matching. If users

Official PDF documents containing the Conduct, Discipline and Appeal (CDA) Rules are 
uploaded to the knowledge base. OCR techniques are applied wherever required to 
extract textual information from scanned documents. The processed documents are

Prompt engineering techniques are employed to ensure that responses are concise, 
professional, and well-structured. The chatbot is also customized with an intuitive user

3. SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE 
3.1 Overall System Architecture 
The Enterprise AI-Powered Knowledge Retrieval System is designed using a modular 
architecture that integrates document processing, intelligent information retrieval, and 
Large Language Model (LLM)-based response generation. The architecture enables users 
to interact with official BCCL documents through a conversational interface while 
ensuring that responses are generated only after retrieving relevant information from the 
organization's knowledge base. 
The system consists of several interconnected components, each responsible for a specific 
task. Initially, official BCCL documents, including the Conduct, Discipline and Appeal 
(CDA) Rules, are uploaded to the knowledge base. If a document is scanned, Optical 
Character Recognition (OCR) is applied to extract machine-readable text. The extracted 
content is then indexed to facilitate efficient semantic retrieval. 
Users interact with the system through the Botpress webchat interface by submitting 
queries in natural language. The Botpress Autonomous Node interprets the query and 
searches the indexed knowledge base to retrieve the most relevant document chunks. 
These retrieved sections are provided as contextual information to the Large Language 
Model, which generates a concise, structured, and context-aware response using carefully 
designed prompt engineering techniques. 
Finally, the generated response is presented to the user through the chatbot interface, 
enabling quick and efficient access to official organizational information.

3.2 System Workflow 
The workflow begins when the administrator uploads official BCCL documents to the 
knowledge base. During document processing, OCR is applied wherever necessary to 
extract textual information from scanned files. The processed documents are indexed to 
support semantic retrieval. 
Whenever a user submits a question through the chatbot interface, the Botpress 
Autonomous Node processes the query and retrieves the most relevant document sections 
from the knowledge base. The retrieved content is then supplied to the Large Language 
Model using Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), allowing the model to generate 
responses based on official documents rather than relying solely on its pre-trained 
knowledge. 
The generated response is formatted into concise bullet points and presented to the user. 
This workflow ensures accurate, reliable, and document-grounded responses while 
significantly reducing the time required to search through lengthy organizational 
documents.

4. TECHNOLOGIES USED 
The development of the Enterprise AI-Powered Knowledge Retrieval System involved 
the integration of several Artificial Intelligence and software technologies. These 
technologies work together to process organizational documents, retrieve relevant 
information, and generate accurate responses to user queries. The major technologies 
used in the project are described below. 
4.1 Artificial Intelligence (AI) 
Artificial Intelligence (AI) refers to the simulation of human intelligence by computer 
systems, enabling them to perform tasks such as understanding language, reasoning, 
decision-making, and problem-solving. Modern AI systems are capable of analyzing 
large volumes of information and generating meaningful responses based on learned 
knowledge. 
In this project, Artificial Intelligence forms the foundation of the proposed system. AI 
enables the chatbot to understand user queries expressed in natural language, retrieve 
relevant information from official BCCL documents, and generate structured responses. 
Instead of relying on predefined rules or keyword matching, the AI-powered system 
provides intelligent and context-aware assistance, making information retrieval faster and 
more efficient. 
 
4.2 Natural Language Processing (NLP) 
Natural Language Processing (NLP) is a branch of Artificial Intelligence that enables 
computers to understand, interpret, and generate human language. NLP allows users to 
interact with software using everyday language instead of predefined commands. 
The chatbot developed during this internship uses NLP to interpret user questions and 
identify the intent behind each query. Users can ask questions in different ways without 
needing to know the exact terminology used in official documents. This significantly 
improves usability compared to traditional keyword-based document searching and 
provides a more natural interaction experience.

4.3 Large Language Models (LLMs) 
Large Language Models (LLMs) are advanced AI models trained on massive text 
datasets to understand language patterns and generate human-like responses. These 
models possess strong capabilities in language understanding, summarization, question 
answering, and text generation. 
The proposed system utilizes an LLM through the Botpress AI platform to generate 
responses based on the information retrieved from official BCCL documents. Rather than 
generating answers solely from pre-trained knowledge, the model receives contextual 
information from the knowledge base before producing the final response. This approach 
improves both the relevance and reliability of the generated answers. 
 
4.4 Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) 
Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) is an AI technique that combines information 
retrieval with Large Language Models to improve the accuracy of generated responses. 
Instead of relying only on the model's internal knowledge, RAG first retrieves relevant 
information from a trusted knowledge source and then uses that information to generate 
the final response. 
In this project, RAG plays a central role in ensuring that chatbot responses remain 
grounded in official BCCL documents. When a user submits a query, the system retrieves 
the most relevant sections from the indexed knowledge base and supplies them to the 
language model. This approach minimizes hallucinations, improves factual accuracy, and 
ensures that responses are based on authentic organizational information. 
 
4.5 Botpress AI Platform 
Botpress is an enterprise conversational AI platform that enables developers to build 
intelligent chatbots with minimal coding effort. It provides features such as workflow 
automation, knowledge base integration, AI-powered autonomous nodes, prompt 
configuration, and webchat deployment. 
The Botpress platform was used as the primary development environment for this project. 
Official BCCL documents were uploaded to the Botpress Knowledge Base, where they 
were indexed for semantic retrieval. The Autonomous Node was configured with custom 
instructions to retrieve relevant document content and generate concise, structured 
responses. The chatbot was then deployed using Botpress Webchat, providing users with 
an interactive interface for accessing organizational information.

4.6 Optical Character Recognition (OCR) 
Optical Character Recognition (OCR) is a document processing technology used to 
convert scanned images or image-based PDF documents into machine-readable text. 
OCR enables search engines and AI systems to process documents that would otherwise 
be inaccessible. 
During the development of the knowledge base, OCR was used wherever necessary to 
extract textual information from scanned PDF documents before indexing. This ensured 
that both digitally generated PDFs and scanned documents could be included in the 
knowledge base, improving the overall coverage of the system. 
 
4.7 Prompt Engineering 
Prompt Engineering is the process of designing and optimizing instructions provided to a 
Large Language Model to improve the quality, consistency, and accuracy of generated 
responses. 
In this project, prompt engineering was used to configure the Botpress Autonomous 
Node. The chatbot was instructed to retrieve information only from the uploaded 
knowledge base, generate concise responses, avoid copying large paragraphs directly, 
and present information using headings and bullet points. Additional prompts were 
designed to encourage professional language, mention rule numbers whenever available, 
and politely guide users after each response. 
 
4.8 Knowledge Base 
A Knowledge Base is a centralized repository that stores documents and information for 
intelligent retrieval. Instead of relying on static question-answer pairs, modern AI 
systems retrieve relevant document sections dynamically from the knowledge base before 
generating responses. 
The knowledge base created for this project contains the official BCCL Conduct, 
Discipline and Appeal (CDA) Rules in PDF format. Once uploaded, the documents were 
processed, indexed, and made searchable through semantic retrieval. This allows the

5. SYSTEM IMPLEMENTATION 
5.1 Overview 
The implementation of the Enterprise AI-Powered Knowledge Retrieval System 
developed during the internship at Bharat Coking Coal Limited (BCCL). The system was 
designed to enable users to retrieve information from official BCCL documents using 
natural language queries through an intelligent chatbot interface. 
The implementation was carried out using the Botpress AI platform, where official 
BCCL Conduct, Discipline and Appeal (CDA) Rules were uploaded to a centralized 
Knowledge Base. The system employs Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) to 
retrieve relevant document content before generating responses using a Large Language 
Model (LLM). Optical Character Recognition (OCR) was utilized for processing 
scanned documents, while Prompt Engineering was applied to improve the quality and 
consistency of chatbot responses. In addition, CSS customization was performed to 
enhance the appearance and user experience of the chatbot interface. 
The implementation process included knowledge base creation, chatbot workflow 
configuration, autonomous node setup, response optimization, interface customization, 
testing, and deployment. Each stage of the implementation is discussed in the following 
sections. 
5.2 Bot Creation and Initial Configuration 
The implementation of the project began with the creation of a new chatbot using the 
Botpress Cloud platform. A dedicated bot named BCCL Rules Assistant was created to 
serve as an AI-powered assistant for retrieving information from official BCCL 
documents. Botpress was selected because of its support for Large Language Models 
(LLMs), Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), knowledge base integration, workflow 
automation, and web-based deployment. 
After creating the bot, the initial project settings were configured, including the bot name, 
description, and AI model. The chatbot workflow was then designed using Botpress 
Studio, where different nodes were organized to manage user interactions and process 
queries efficiently. The Autonomous Node was selected as the primary processing 
component, allowing the chatbot to understand user queries, retrieve relevant information 
from the knowledge base, and generate context-aware responses. 
This initial configuration established the foundation for the subsequent stages of 
development, including knowledge base integration, prompt engineering, interface 
customization, testing, and deployment.

5.3 Knowledge Base Creation 
The Knowledge Base serves as the core repository of the proposed Enterprise AI-
Powered Knowledge Retrieval System. It contains the official BCCL documents that are 
used by the chatbot to retrieve relevant information while answering user queries. During 
the implementation phase, the Conduct, Discipline and Appeal (CDA) Rules and other 
relevant organizational documents were uploaded to the Botpress Knowledge Base. 
Once the documents were uploaded, Botpress automatically processed and indexed their 
contents to enable semantic search. For scanned PDF documents, Optical Character 
Recognition (OCR) was applied to extract machine-readable text before indexing. The 
processed documents were then divided into smaller searchable segments, commonly 
referred to as chunks, allowing the system to retrieve only the most relevant portions of 
the document instead of searching the entire file. 
The indexed knowledge base enables the chatbot to understand the context of user 
queries and retrieve the most relevant document sections before generating a response. 
This approach improves the accuracy of the answers while ensuring that the information 
is derived from official BCCL documents. As a result, users can obtain reliable and 
document-grounded responses without manually searching through lengthy PDF files.

5.4 Autonomous Node Configuration 
The Autonomous Node acts as the central processing unit of the chatbot and is 
responsible for understanding user queries, retrieving relevant information from the 
knowledge base, and generating appropriate responses. Unlike traditional rule-based 
chatbots that rely on predefined conversation flows, the Autonomous Node uses Artificial 
Intelligence to dynamically interpret user intent and respond based on the information 
available in the uploaded BCCL documents. 
To improve the quality and consistency of responses, custom instructions were provided 
to the Autonomous Node through prompt engineering. The node was configured to 
always search the uploaded knowledge base before generating a response, summarize 
retrieved information instead of copying large paragraphs, present answers using 
headings and bullet points, mention rule numbers whenever available, and respond 
professionally. If the requested information was not found in the uploaded documents, the 
chatbot was instructed to inform the user accordingly instead of generating unsupported 
information. 
This configuration enabled the chatbot to provide concise, accurate, and document-
grounded responses while maintaining a consistent response format. The Autonomous 
Node significantly enhanced the chatbot's ability to answer a wide range of rule-related 
queries in a natural and user-friendly manner.

5.5 Prompt Engineering 
Prompt Engineering played a significant role in improving the quality, consistency, and 
reliability of the chatbot's responses. Since the chatbot utilizes a Large Language Model 
(LLM), carefully designed instructions were provided to guide its behavior and ensure 
that responses remained accurate, professional, and based on official BCCL documents. 
The prompt was configured to instruct the chatbot to always retrieve information from 
the uploaded Knowledge Base before generating a response. It was also designed to 
summarize retrieved content instead of copying lengthy paragraphs directly from the 
documents. To improve readability, responses were formatted using clear headings and 
bullet points, while rule numbers were included whenever available. Additionally, the 
chatbot was instructed to respond professionally, avoid generating unsupported 
information, and inform the user whenever the requested information could not be found 
in the available documents. 
Several iterations of prompt refinement were carried out during the implementation 
process to improve response quality and maintain consistency across different user 
queries. These refinements helped the chatbot generate more structured, context-aware, 
and user-friendly responses while ensuring that the information remained grounded in the 
official BCCL knowledge base.

7.1 Challenges Faced 
During the development of the Enterprise AI-Powered Knowledge Retrieval System, 
several technical and implementation-related challenges were encountered. These 
challenges provided valuable learning opportunities and contributed to improving the 
overall quality of the chatbot. 
One of the primary challenges was processing PDF documents for the knowledge base. 
Some documents were scanned copies, making them difficult to search directly. Optical 
Character Recognition (OCR) was required to extract machine-readable text before the 
documents could be indexed for semantic retrieval. 
Another challenge involved configuring the chatbot to generate accurate and concise 
responses. Initially, the chatbot occasionally produced lengthy outputs or responses that 
were not properly structured. This issue was addressed through prompt engineering by 
providing clear instructions to summarize information, use bullet points, mention rule 
numbers where available, and maintain a professional tone. 
The knowledge base indexing process also required careful validation to ensure that 
uploaded documents were processed successfully before the chatbot could retrieve 
information. During testing, multiple iterations of prompt refinement and chatbot 
configuration were performed to improve response quality and maintain consistency 
across different user queries. 
In addition, customizing the Botpress web interface required modifications to the default 
CSS styles to create a more professional and user-friendly appearance suitable for an 
enterprise application. These challenges collectively enhanced the understanding of 
enterprise AI systems, conversational AI, and knowledge management technologies.
